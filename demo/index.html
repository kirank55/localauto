Localauto beta

Try a fill without leaving your machine

  1. Load the extension from .output/chrome-mv3.
  2. Open the side panel from the toolbar icon.
  3. Enter a profile and open one of the forms below.
  4. Click Fill empty fields. Suggestions wait for Accept.
  5. Answer a custom question, choose Find answers to save, then confirm.
  6. Pick a personality above the profile, or create one. Default stays selected until you choose another. Delete a profile in Settings.
  7. Open the submit demo and press Submit. The side panel opens with those details so you can press Save profile.
